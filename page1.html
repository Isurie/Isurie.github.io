Sinhalese Language Based Hate Speech Detection and Intent Analysis on Tweets

Research into detecting Sinhalese-language hate speech and analysing tweet intent using semantic analysis, text preprocessing and data-mining techniques.

01 / RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Turning Sinhala text into meaningful insights.

The research explored the development of a tool for detecting Sinhalese-language hate speech and analysing the intention of tweets.

Research focus

Semantic analysis and data-mining techniques were used to address the challenge of understanding and classifying Sinhala-language social media content.

My contribution

My contribution included implementing a text preprocessing module whose outputs were used as inputs for analysis. I also worked on classifying Sinhala texts found on Twitter into primary domains and subjective categories.

Research objective

The classification process aimed to support further analysis of hate according to domain and subject classes, helping define the severity level of tweet content.

Project Level 4 research project
Research area Natural Language Processing
Language Sinhala
Techniques Semantic analysis and data mining
Tools Python, NLTK, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn
