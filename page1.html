RESEARCH PROJECT · 2020 — 2021

Sinhalese Language Based Hate Speech Detection and Intent Analysis on Tweets

A B.Sc. Level 4 research project exploring detection of Sinhalese-language hate speech and analysis of tweet intent using semantic analysis and data-mining techniques.

Python NLTK Pandas NumPy Scikit-learn PyCharm

01 / OVERVIEW

From text preprocessing to intent analysis.

The project focused on developing a tool to detect Sinhalese language-based hate speech and analyse the intention of tweets.

The work used semantic analysis and data-mining techniques to address the problem.

My contribution

My contribution included implementing a text preprocessing module whose outcomes were used as inputs for analysis, classifying Sinhala texts found on Twitter into primary domains, and further classifying Sinhala texts into subjective categories.

02 / MODULES

01

Twitter text analysis

Extracting and preprocessing Sinhala Twitter text for downstream analysis.

02

Context classification

Classifying tweet content across domains such as religion, racist, political, sports and sexism, plus other content.

03

Subject classification

Classifying Sinhala texts into opinion, news and emotion categories.

04

Hate & intent analysis

Using classified domain and subject information to support further analysis of hate and intent.
